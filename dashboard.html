What this page is

Recorded runs, not a live system. Every answer, citation, verdict and excerpt below came out of the real pipeline against the real index on 2026-08-23, using claude-sonnet-4-5. Nothing is illustrative and nothing was edited.

It is recorded because the app cannot honestly be deployed: it needs a 373 MB vector index and 3.6 GB of source filings that do not belong in a repository, and a public endpoint would put an API key behind an unmetered text box. Recording it costs USD 0.184 once and nothing thereafter.

Where the guardrail sits

Two gates and one check. The gates decide whether a question reaches the model at all; the check decides whether its answer reaches you as written. Both are ground truth against the retrieved excerpts, not a second model's opinion.
